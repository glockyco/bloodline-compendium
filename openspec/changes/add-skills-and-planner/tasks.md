- [ ] 1.1 Trace the skill window's bound destinations, level-based slots and passive fields, and determine canonical discipline, passive and spellsong source identifiers. Acceptance: a local catalog audit maps every reachable discipline button to its tree, records Biomancy opening Arcane, identifies every slot by its owning asset and source identifier, and lists unresolved associations in coverage without display-name joins.
- [ ] 1.2 Trace skill unlock writes and consumers and observe repeated unlock attempts and reopening a tree through the lab. Acceptance: recorded runs identify where an unlock is held and its identity, show one purchase charging once, and establish idempotent allocation inputs before allocatable skills are published. If persistence remains unresolved, the affected unlock stays visibly unknown rather than being represented as valid.
- [ ] 1.3 Record fresh-character baselines, character-level grants, discipline-level grants, attribute increase and decrease behavior, level domains and passive thresholds and rank caps using bound handlers and game functions. Acceptance: repeat runs establish starting levels and point pools, 1 skill point and 3 level up points per character level, 1 proficiency point per discipline level, attribute refunds and the insufficient-point and maximum-rank boundaries. Unestablished floors or caps appear as unknown inputs.
- [ ] 1.4 Recover and recheck the earlier character experience vectors and measure discipline experience requirements and gain adjustments. Acceptance: build artifacts distinguish character and discipline tracks, reproduce Pyromancy 152 at level 1, 156 at level 2 and a 50 award counting as 51 on the matching build, or record changed-build differences with ownership. Checks cover further levels and rounding boundaries, and any unverified domain remains unknown.
- [ ] 1.5 Trace armor proficiency tracking and record controlled damage observations for armor classes and an unarmored control through the lab. Acceptance: separate run records explain which proficiency changes, or coverage names the unresolved gain rule. No armor planner inputs, equipment controls or mobility totals are added.
- [ ] 1.6 Trace passive, attribute and spellsong effect consumers and any reachable skill or passive refund actions. Acceptance: measured effects and refunds have recorded results and citations, unsupported calculations remain unknown, and each disagreement is assigned to its producer, the tool, a reproduced game issue or coverage under the existing ownership contract.

Acceptance: browser tests allocate and undo attributes, enforce skill thresholds and passive costs, expose deficits after level reductions, show unknown requirements, and distinguish removing a planned selection from an in-game refund.

Acceptance: invented round-trip tests preserve valid progression state and reject malformed data, unsupported versions, duplicate allocations, invalid levels and ranks, encoded fragments over 8 KiB and expanded builds over 64 KiB before unbounded expansion. - [ ] 5.3 Add shared-build previews and explicit save and activation actions. Acceptance: opening a link changes neither stored characters nor active selection, cross-build previews identify their source and revalidate against the current catalog, and saving or activation occurs only after the corresponding explicit action.
- [ ] 5.4 Add a versioned browser character store with create, name, edit, duplicate, select and delete actions. Acceptance: browser tests retain several independent characters and active selection across reloads, handle understood migrations, and exercise corrupt, unsupported, unavailable and full storage without discarding readable entries or claiming an unsuccessful save persisted.
- [ ] 5.5 Add active-character cues to applicable reference pages through a minimal page-specific context projection. Acceptance: selecting and switching characters updates level and affordability cues consistently with the planner, clearing selection removes them, no-JavaScript pages remain complete, and prerendered facts, canonical metadata and search content stay neutral.
- [ ] 5.6 Enforce planner payload isolation and delivery budgets. Acceptance: browser network checks show no full planner request on neutral reference navigation or context activation, the planner loads its separate payload within 512 KiB, and over-budget payload and link fixtures fail their gates with the measured size and limit.
